For restaurant owners

Card tips paid to your team every week, with the working shown.

Sciar Tips takes the card tips from your terminal, splits them by the rule you set, and pays your staff each week. Each person can see how their own payout was worked out.

  • Every cent of card tips went to the team. In our 12-week pilot at one Dublin restaurant, €31,754.13 in card tips came in and €31,754.09 was paid out to staff. The 4 cents is rounding.1
  • Paid weekly, every week of the pilot. 12 weekly payouts in 12 weeks, to 13 staff across five roles.2
  • Staff see their own working, and can ask privately. In the alpha, each person sees their payout, the hours that earned points and the sum behind it, and can write a private question and see it listed with a reference (stored on their own phone in the alpha).3

Sciar Tips is in alpha with one pilot restaurant. We would rather show you the numbers than make promises.
